What's inside

Every studio is its own oshal application, installed from the app store. Create puts them on one rail and reads their saved work onto one page — it never copies a studio or generates anything itself.

How it works

Pick a starter

Presentation, document, spreadsheet, headshot, character, group shot, short video, clip, story episode or a LoRA character. Search or filter by kind.

The studio opens ready

Every starter opens the studio that owns it — the same rail, the same skin, your same account. Nothing to install per studio once it is in your swarm.

Pick up where you left off

Continue creating gathers your saved work from every studio onto one page, newest first, with the counts each studio keeps for itself.

Built the oshal way

  • The studios own their domains. Create is a launcher with a face — a manifest, a bundled skin, a home page and two static routes. It stores nothing and calls no model.
  • Your session, your data. The home page asks each studio's own Home probe in your signed-in session. A probe that cannot answer says so; it never invents a number.
  • Nothing is sent from the home page. Generation, rendering and publishing happen in the studio you open, behind that studio's own approvals and cost gates.
  • Your infrastructure, your keys. Runs on the swarm you install — laptop, on-prem or cloud — with the providers you connect.
  • Open source. The package lives in the public app store under AGPL-3.0-or-later; install it on your own swarm from there.
