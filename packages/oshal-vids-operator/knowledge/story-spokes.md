# The 5 spokes — video archetypes

Each pushed story is one of these shapes. Build to the archetype: the structure
sets the shot list, the style sets the look + audio + graphics. (The registry in
`src/stories/spokes.js` is the source of truth; this grounds the operating agent.)

1. **Sports review** — hook on the best highlight, 1-2 key plays, a SCORE/STATS
   card (Text/Image layer, final score + key numbers), quick recap. Broadcast-
   energetic: punchy motion, slow-mo on the big play, bold score graphics, upbeat
   bed with a hit on the moment.

2. **Whiteboard explainer** — intro the question, then 2-4 shots each drawing ONE
   idea building on the last, recap the assembled diagram. Hand-drawn marker on a
   white board, simple icons/arrows, calm pace, labels as Text layers.

3. **Stick-figure joke** — setup the character/situation, escalate, land the
   punchline held a beat (+ optional kicker caption). Minimal stick-figure
   animation, exaggerated timing, a beat of silence before the punchline, light
   comedic sting.

4. **Blog / vlog** — intro/hook title card + opening shot, 1-2 story shots, a
   wrap-up, then an END CARD CTA ("click here for part 2" / subscribe) as a Text
   layer. Warm, personal, casual handheld feel, mellow bed.

5. **Brand promo** — striking brand hook, value/product in motion, payoff that
   resolves to the logo + a tagline Text layer. Premium/cinematic, one consistent
   palette across shots, slow moves, clean logo reveal, refined swell.

Across all: one palette per video, never bake words/logos into a Veo clip (layer
them with Text/Image), Insert for cuts and Extend to lengthen a beat, music bed
trimmed to length and faded out.
