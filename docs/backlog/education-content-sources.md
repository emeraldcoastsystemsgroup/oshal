# Backlog: Education Content Sources & Search

## Problem
When getting tutored, students need access to the best educational content — not just what's in their textbook. The tutor should be able to reference and link to high-quality free learning resources.

## Potential Sources
- **Khan Academy** — free, covers K-12, has video + practice problems
- **OpenStax** — free textbooks (CC-licensed), covers most subjects
- **CK-12** — free FlexBooks and practice
- **MIT OpenCourseWare** — older students, college-level
- **Crash Course (YouTube)** — engaging video explanations
- **PhET Interactive Simulations** — science/math simulations (Colorado)
- **Desmos** — graphing calculator + activities
- **Quizlet** — public flashcard sets (millions available)
- **LibreTexts** — open textbooks

## Implementation Ideas
1. **Search tool for education bots** — `search-educational-content` tool that queries these sources
2. **ChromaDB knowledge bootstrap** — pre-load common curriculum content into global collections
3. **Tutor citation enrichment** — when tutor answers, append relevant links: "Learn more: Khan Academy video on X"
4. **Resource library per class** — teacher adds links, students browse

## Priority
High — directly impacts tutoring quality and student learning outcomes.
