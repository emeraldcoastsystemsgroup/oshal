Shared knowledge

What every bot here can retrieve. A document in the shared corpus belongs to the swarm and answers for everyone; a document filed under one bot is meant for that bot; a person's private document answers only for them. What you add on this screen goes into the shared corpus. Removing a document removes its record and, for documents stored since chunks carried an id, its chunks; an older document keeps its chunks until its collection is deleted, and its row says so.
