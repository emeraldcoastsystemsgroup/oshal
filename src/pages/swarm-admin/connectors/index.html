Connectors

The connectors this swarm offers. Enabling one publishes its audited definition and its tools to the bots here; where a connector needs a sign-in, each person signs in with their own account unless the connector declares a deployment credential. A person may switch a connector off for themselves. A connector that fails its audit stays blocked until the audit passes.
