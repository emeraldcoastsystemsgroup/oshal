Devices

Every computer and node that has joined this swarm, whoever it is bound to. A node runs a person's own work only when it is bound to them; no person's work is dispatched to an unbound node, though operators and platform jobs can still reach it. A person adds their own computer from their Devices tool, which binds it to their account; here you can bind or unbind any device and rotate its node token.
