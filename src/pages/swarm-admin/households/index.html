Households

Every household, team and company of this swarm, with its members from this swarm's directory and their roles. A household is managed by its own admins: you can change the members of the ones you are an admin of, and of any you create here. The others you can see, and review a member's access, but not change. People from an external identity provider are counted here and managed on the access screen. Each person's access review shows everything they may reach.
