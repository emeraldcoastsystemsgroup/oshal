Ambient Recall

Ask what someone said. Answers are counted from your own transcripts — the number is exact, the lines are quoted verbatim, and playback re-speaks them in the assistant voice. Topics, asks and trends are OSHAL's read of those words, always shown beside them, and can be deleted. No audio is ever stored.

Try: count a topic · what did they say · open asks

Asks and commitments are inferences — OSHAL's read of what was said — and each one is shown beside the words it came from so you can judge it. Marking one done is your call, never the model's.
