The problem

AI today is fragmented and locked-in.

Every vendor wants you on their model for everything. But no single model is best — or cheapest — at every task. Pinning a whole business to one AI stack means you overpay for simple work, you can't shop for quality, and your data lives in someone else's box.

🔒

Locked in

A single-vendor stack can't route you off its own models — even when a rival is cheaper or better.

💸

Wasteful

Running a top-tier model on trivial tasks burns budget. One size never fits all.

🧩

Disconnected

Your email, files, calendar, socials and accounts don't talk to your AI.

What OSHAL is

A swarm of specialized AI workers.

Instead of one giant assistant, OSHAL runs many focused bots. A controller takes a request, breaks it into phases, and dispatches each phase to the bot built for it. Bots collaborate over a shared message bus — like a team, not a monolith.

🎛️

The controller

Routes work, tracks cost, enforces approvals. It orchestrates — it never calls an AI model itself.

🤖

The bots

Each bot owns a domain and runs its own AI engine — Claude Code, Codex, Cline, Gemini…

🔗

The connectors

Bots act on your real accounts through per-user, encrypted tokens you grant once.

The differentiator

Route every task to the best AI for it.

We call it the Token Chase: for any business process, OSHAL can match the work to the right tool, the right model, and the right platform — optimizing cost and quality. Because OSHAL is vendor-neutral, nothing stops it from moving you to whatever wins.

How it works

Three layers, one clean separation.

01 · ORCHESTRATE

Controller

Accepts your request, splits it into phases, and dispatches each to the right bot. Captures cost and runs approval gates. No AI calls here.

→
02 · EXECUTE

Bot nodes

Each bot runs its chosen AI tool + model, does the reasoning, and calls real APIs. This is where all the intelligence — and all the cost — lives.

→
03 · ACT

Your accounts

Through connectors you authorize, bots read and act on your email, files, calendar, socials and devices — on your behalf, never beyond your scopes.
